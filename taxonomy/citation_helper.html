Citation helper

Paste BibTeX, get one citation line per entry for the citation column of the screening tab.

Format: FirstAuthorSurname et al. Title. Venue Year
One author: Surname. Two authors: Surname and Surname. Three or more: Surname et al. Common venues get a fixed short name (NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, KDD, ACM CCS, USENIX Security, NDSS, IEEE S&P and others). Other venues use the acronym in parentheses when the entry has one, otherwise the full name.

Results appear here. Each line can be edited before copying.
